1

Cut

The sender slices the message into small packets. Each one is stamped with a sequence number, a destination and a hop limit.

2

Route

Every router picks the next hop on its own, weighing distance, bandwidth and how long its queues are. Packets of one message can take different roads.

3

Reassemble

The receiver puts the packets back in order, asks again for any that went missing, and only then shows the message.
